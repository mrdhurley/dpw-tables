How It Works

Best For
Floating & Decorative Fountains A surface unit that sprays water into the air for movement and a visible display Entrances and common areas that need movement, circulation, and a stronger first impression
Pond & Lake Aeration Systems Adds oxygen and keeps the water mixing from top to bottom Ponds with odor, frequent algae, soft bottom muck, or fish gulping at the surface
Submerged Pond Diffusers Bottom-mounted diffusers release fine bubbles that mix the full water column Ponds that need oxygen while the surface stays calm
Stormwater Pond Aeration Diffused air or surface mixing that keeps oxygen levels up in ponds that treat runoff Stormwater ponds with recurring odor, algae, or heavy sludge
