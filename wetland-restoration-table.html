Service

What It Does
Studies & Permitting
Wetland Delineation Identifies wetland boundaries so projects can move forward with confidence
Pond & Lake Sediment Studies Measures sediment depth, composition, and accumulation to guide dredge and restoration plans
Wetland & Waterway Permitting and Jurisdictional Determinations Clarifies which rules apply and supports the permits a project needs
Environmental Site Assessments (Phase I & II) Identifies environmental conditions that could affect a property
Rare & Threatened Species Determination Confirms whether protected species are present so work can be planned around them
Restoration & Construction
Wetland Restoration, Creation & Channeling Rebuilds or creates wetland areas that filter water and support native habitat
Conservation Landscape Planning & Construction Plans and installs native landscapes that hold soil and support wildlife
Land & Habitat Management
Riparian Buffer Management Maintains the planted strip along a shoreline that filters runoff and holds the bank
Conservation Area, Easement & Forest Management Keeps protected land healthy and in line with easement requirements
Invasive Species Control for Natural Areas Removes invasive plants so native vegetation can recover
Right-of-Way Vegetation Management Keeps corridors clear for access, safety, and maintenance
Drone Herbicide Application Reaches hard-to-access areas with targeted treatment
Prescribed Burning Renews native plant communities and reduces invasive growth
Community & Funding
Educational Outreach Programs Helps community members understand and care for local water and land
Grant Writing Helps secure funding for restoration and conservation projects
